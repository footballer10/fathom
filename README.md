# Fathom

### 🌊 **[Play Fathom → play.fathomgame.workers.dev](https://play.fathomgame.workers.dev)**

A free deep-sea trivia game. You get a prompt like **"Name a fruit"** and type one answer before
the timer runs out. Obvious answers barely get you wet; rare ones send your submarine plunging
into the deep. Every point sinks you 10 meters, from the Sunlight Zone down to the Abyss.

Free to play, no ads, no download. Works on phones, tablets and computers.

---

## How it works

Every prompt has a long list of valid answers, and each one has a rarity tier:

| Tier | Points | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| 🦐 Plankton | 10 | The first thing almost everyone says |
| 🎣 Too Clever | 15 | A "clever" pick that lots of people think of |
| 🐟 Schooler | 30 | Common, but not the very first answer |
| 🐚 Rare | 60 | You know it, but most people won't say it |
| 🦑 Deep Cut | 85 | Specialist knowledge |
| 🐋 One in a Krillion | 100 | The single rarest answer on the list |

So for "Name a fruit", *apple* is Plankton, but *persimmon* is Rare and *jabuticaba* is a
One in a Krillion. Misspell something and the game asks "Did you mean…?"

## Ways to play

- **Daily**: the same 7 prompts for everyone, once a day (new Daily at midnight Mountain time).
  See where you landed on the score curve, keep your streak going, share your results, and compare
  with the day's top divers.
- **Unlimited**: 7 random prompts whenever you like, from every category or just one.
- **Quick modes**: **Sprint** (as deep as you can in 60 seconds), **Ladder** (each answer has to be
  rarer than the last), **Rarity Roulette** (hit the exact tier you're given) and **Five Answers**
  (five answers to one prompt).
- **Weekly Abyss**: one very deep prompt a week and one 45-second dive. Your rarest answer counts.
- **Soccer**: a fan category with 200+ questions about the Premier League, European competitions
  and international tournaments. It only shows up when you pick it.

## Party mode

Make a room, share the four-letter code, and play live with up to 16 friends.

- **Dive**: everyone answers the same prompt, then the subs race to the bottom.
  Variants: **Co-op** (one shared submarine with a team depth goal), **Shark Tank** (the most
  common answer gets bitten) and **School of Fish** (points for matching your friends).
- **Battle**: take turns answering with no repeats while everyone watches you type; run out of
  lives and you're out. Variants: **Hot Potato** and **Relay** (two teams, where each answer has to
  start with the last letter of your teammate's).

## Pearls, quests and the shop

Every game pays pearls: the Daily, Unlimited dives, quick modes, party games and the Weekly
Abyss. Daily and weekly quests pay more, and Daily streaks pay the most, with special items at
7, 14, 30, 50, 100 and 365 days that you can't buy. Spend pearls in the shop on submarine paint
jobs, bubble trails, sidekicks that swim beside your sub, celebrations for rare answers, new ocean
colours, titles and name colours. Your friends see your sub and title in parties and on the
leaderboards. Nothing in the shop changes your score.

## Logbook

Your logbook keeps every Daily you've played on a calendar (tap a day to see your answers), your
rarest finds, your lifetime numbers and how your answers spread across the tiers.

## Leaderboards and friends

Sign up with a username and password (no email needed) to keep your progress on any device,
appear on the global leaderboards, and add friends by code. Or just play as a guest.
